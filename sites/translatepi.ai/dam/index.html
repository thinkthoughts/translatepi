01 / Reading point

From memory energy to an expected gap

The starting point is the generalized associative-memory energy in Krotov and Hopfield's Dense Associative Memory for Pattern Recognition. A stored binary pattern is tested against a one-neuron flip. In Lean, the finite calculation is divided into a deterministic signal and the contributions from other stored patterns.

E(σ) = −∑μ F(∑i ξiμσi) Polynomial instance: F(x) = xn

The specification keeps distinct the mathematical energy, asynchronous update rule, finite probability model, asymptotic arguments, and any physical implementation.

02 / Verified progression

Three exact steps

CP6b

Decompose

The one-flip energy gap is an exact signal plus non-target cross-talk.

CP6c.1

Cancel

Cross-talk has conditional mean zero under the finite uniform binary-pattern model.

CP6c.2

Average

The full conditional expected gap equals the deterministic polynomial signal.

Exact one-flip energy

ΔEiμ = [Nn − (N − 2)n] + ηiμ

The selected memory has overlap N with itself and N − 2 after one coordinate is flipped. The remaining memories supply the cross-talk term η.

CP6c.1 / Conditional cross-talk

𝔼[ηiμ | ξμ = t] = 0

The proof pairs configurations using a coordinate-toggle involution. For a non-target memory, the toggle reverses its energy-difference summand while preserving the target pattern. This cancellation holds for an arbitrary separation function F under the stated finite symmetry conditions; it does not assert that each cross-talk sample is zero.

CP6c.2 / Exact conditional expectation

𝔼[ΔEiμ | ξμ = t] = Nn − (N − 2)n

For polyF n, finite-sum linearity removes the mean-zero cross-talk and preserves the constant signal. The subtraction is interpreted in the real numbers, including networks with one or two neurons. No asymptotic or Gaussian reasoning is required.

05 / Leading constraints

What follows—and what remains open

Verified finite layer

  • Exact binary one-flip mechanics and energy descent.
  • Deterministic signal/noise decomposition.
  • Symmetry-based conditional mean-zero cross-talk.
  • Exact conditional expected gap for polynomial separation.

Next reading points

  • CP6c.3: exact finite second moment and noise variance.
  • Certified retrieval-error tail bounds.
  • Hypothesis-bearing capacity scaling and asymptotics.
  • Separate numerical and physical-implementation refinements.

A mean-zero interference term can fluctuate substantially; its expectation alone proves neither retrieval success nor a storage-capacity bound. Likewise, mathematical energy identities do not measure chip power, latency, or throughput.
