01 / Mathematical objects

From constraints to exact integrals

The development specifies constrained simplex supports and piecewise cap conditions, constructs a rational polynomial basis, and derives exact integral formulas. The results are formalized in Lean 4 with explicit theorem dependencies and independently checkable certificates.

The polynomial basis

The 846-dimensional B₂₁ basis, with polynomial generators built from a slack power and power-sum factors.

Exact integration

General monomial formulas for IT, with arbitrary cap functions and a worked exact rational Gram entry.

Two-aggregate integration · V2e-A

Weighted high-dimensional simplex integrals reduce to two aggregate variables. Rational polynomial forms and a worked certificate of 19/7680 are kernel-checked.

Polygon-cell integration · V2e-B

Exact rational polynomial integrals over clipped two-aggregate cells, with a verified cap-branch formula and a Table 3 certificate for one specified contribution to JT.

Exact marginal Gram entry · V2f

All endpoint branches integrated and assembled: the constant-generator entry JT[1,1] ≈ 1.1193487646 × 10⁻⁵⁵, certified exactly from 133 rational pieces.

Bilinear power-sum Gram entry · V2g

The first nonconstant marginal entry JT[P₂,1] ≈ 4.99943968764 × 10⁻⁵⁷, certified using 133 rational pieces and 266 exact lemmas. Coordinate placement and shifted power sums are formalized.

Power-sum-square Gram entry · V2h

The diagonal entry JT[P₂,P₂] ≈ 2.27107 × 10⁻⁵⁸ completes the 2×2 block. Two-coordinate placements, diagonal terms, and shared moment-table certificates are verified by Lean.

General marginal Gram entries · V2i

For arbitrary generators Ga,λ = (L − P₁)aPλ, Lean proves a rational marginal Gram-entry representation. The Table 3 theorem covers every generator pair in B₂₁; selected entries are evaluated with exact certificates.

Shared-moment aggregation · V2j

Canonical moment keys collect repeated contributions across Gram-entry combinations. Lean proves the aggregation agrees exactly with V2i, with four compiled demonstrations and a 24-statement Comparator accepted independently on the MacBook Pro and Linux VPS.

Symmetric-function reduction · V2k

A canonical augmented-monomial symmetric-function evaluator replaces V2i’s placement recursion. Lean proves general equivalence with V2i’s placement recursion and verifies exact coefficient tables, including an 85-coefficient piece demonstration. Its 36-statement Comparator suite was accepted on Mac and Linux VPS.

Staged symmetric-function certificates · V2ℓ

Lean proves that certified step chains, chunks, and merges recover V2k’s evaluator and V2i’s placement value. Factor-order invariance permits prefix reuse; certified profiles give a 231-moment representation for the degree-12 Table 3 piece (6,3). The 35-statement Comparator passed on the Linux VPS.

Marginal-fibre geometry

The exact piecewise fibre endpoint for JT, empty-fibre theorems, marginal identity, and inner polynomial integral, independently verified on the VPS.

Bounded prime gaps

Combinatorial packing and the verified conditional implication from DHL[45,2] to H1 ≤ 212; additional analytic and variational obligations remain.

02 / Verified mathematics

A verified implication and its research pathway

V2f–V2h certify the symmetric 2×2 marginal Gram block on {1, P₂}. V2i proves an exact rational representation for every pair of B₂₁ generators, and V2j proves shared-moment aggregation preserves that representation. V2k proves equivalence of a symmetric-function evaluator to V2i’s placement recursion. V2ℓ proves staged certificate composition, factor-order invariance, and reusable moment profiles, with degree-12 demonstrations compiled on Mac and VPS and a 35-statement Comparator accepted on Linux. The full numerical 846×846 matrix and variational positivity remain open work.

03 / Exact marginal Gram block

Three certified entries, one polynomial pair

For the functions 1 and P₂ = ∑ᵢxᵢ², the marginal bilinear form has a rational 2×2 Gram block. Decimal values below are readable approximations of the exact rational certificates.

GJ on (1, P₂)
1.11935 × 10−554.99944 × 10−57 4.99944 × 10−572.27107 × 10−58
The numerical Cauchy–Schwarz ratio JT[P₂,1]² / (JT[1,1] · JT[P₂,P₂]) ≈ 0.984. The matrix entries are certified individually; a separate exact determinant-positivity certificate is a further target.

V2h handles diagonal and distinct-coordinate placements. V2i generalizes the placement construction to products of arbitrary power sums and certifies further Table 3 entries: JT[P₄,1] ≈ 2.75169 × 10−59, JT[P₂²,1] ≈ 2.28799 × 10−58, and JT[L−P₁,1] ≈ 5.52809 × 10−57. These approximations correspond to exact rationals in the Lean certificates.
