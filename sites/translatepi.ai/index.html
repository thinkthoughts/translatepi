01 / Mathematical objects

From constraints to exact integrals

The development specifies constrained simplex supports and piecewise cap conditions, constructs a rational polynomial basis, and derives exact integral formulas. The results are formalized in Lean 4 with explicit theorem dependencies and independently checkable certificates.

The polynomial basis

The 846-dimensional B₂₁ basis, with polynomial generators built from a slack power and power-sum factors.

Exact integration

General monomial formulas for IT, with arbitrary cap functions and a worked exact rational Gram entry.

Marginal-fibre geometry

The exact piecewise fibre endpoint for JT, empty-fibre theorems, marginal identity, and inner polynomial integral, independently verified on the VPS.

Bounded prime gaps

Combinatorial packing and the verified conditional implication from DHL[45,2] to H1 ≤ 212; additional analytic and variational obligations remain.

02 / A precise distinction

The conditional theorem and its open inputs

DHL[45,2]  ⟹  H1 ≤ 212Formalized conditional implication, not yet an unconditional formalization of the bound.

The ongoing variational work supplies exact integral foundations. General JT evaluation, an optimized polynomial positivity certificate, and remaining analytic hypotheses are separate proof obligations.

04 / Reproducibility

Readable mathematics, checkable evidence

Proof statements and certificates are preserved in the public repository, together with Challenge/Solution pairs, Comparator configurations, negative controls, pinned toolchain information, and result records. All fifteen Comparator suites have passed independent VPS verification, including V2d’s fibre theorem. Its four positive controls were accepted and four negative controls rejected. The V2d proof is recorded at commit 52fd36a, with VPS verification provenance at 90055db.
