Nat.totient_prod_primes

Euler's totient formula for the product of a finite set of distinct primes, offered as a reusable Mathlib API lemma.

Submitted · upstream review

The theorem

/-- Euler's product formula for a product of distinct primes. -/
theorem totient_prod_primes {s : Finset ℕ} (hs : ∀ p ∈ s, p.Prime) :
    φ (∏ p ∈ s, p) = ∏ p ∈ s, (p - 1)

Because s is a finset, its prime elements are distinct. For example, primes {2, 3, 5} give φ(30) = (2 − 1)(3 − 1)(5 − 1) = 8.

Change submitted

The proposed addition is a 12-line change to Mathlib/Data/Nat/Totient.lean, immediately after totient_eq_div_primeFactors_mul. No imports are added. The proof uses Finset.prod_induction with existing totient_mul, totient_prime, coprime_primes, and Coprime.mul_right. The exact proof text is preserved in the linked source commit.
