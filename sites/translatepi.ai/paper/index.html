01 / Mathematical objects

From constraints to exact integrals

The development specifies constrained simplex supports and piecewise cap conditions, constructs a rational polynomial basis, and derives exact integral formulas. The results are formalized in Lean 4 with explicit theorem dependencies and independently checkable certificates.

The polynomial basis · V0

The 846-dimensional B₂₁ basis, with polynomial generators built from a slack power and power-sum factors.

Exact integration · V2c

General monomial formulas for I T , with arbitrary cap functions and a worked exact rational Gram entry.

Marginal-fibre geometry · V2d

The exact piecewise fibre endpoint for J T , empty-fibre theorems, marginal identity, and inner polynomial integral, independently verified on the VPS.

Two-aggregate integration · V2e-A

Weighted high-dimensional simplex integrals reduce to two aggregate variables. Rational polynomial forms and a worked certificate of 19/7680 are kernel-checked.

Polygon-cell integration · V2e-B

Exact rational polynomial integrals over clipped two-aggregate cells, with a verified cap-branch formula and a Table 3 certificate for one specified contribution to J T .

Exact marginal Gram entry · V2f

All endpoint branches integrated and assembled: the constant-generator entry J T [1,1] ≈ 1.1193487646 × 10⁻⁵⁵, certified exactly from 133 rational pieces.

Bilinear power-sum Gram entry · V2g

The first nonconstant marginal entry J T [P₂,1] ≈ 4.99943968764 × 10⁻⁵⁷, certified using 133 rational pieces and 266 exact lemmas. Coordinate placement and shifted power sums are formalized.

Power-sum-square Gram entry · V2h

The diagonal entry J T [P₂,P₂] ≈ 2.27107 × 10⁻⁵⁸ completes the 2×2 block. Two-coordinate placements, diagonal terms, and shared moment-table certificates are verified by Lean.

General marginal Gram entries · V2i

For arbitrary generators G a,λ = (L − P₁) a P λ , Lean proves a rational marginal Gram-entry representation. The Table 3 theorem covers every generator pair in B₂₁; selected entries are evaluated with exact certificates.

Shared-moment aggregation · V2j

Canonical moment keys collect repeated contributions across Gram-entry combinations. Lean proves the aggregation agrees exactly with V2i, with four compiled demonstrations and a 24-statement Comparator accepted independently on the MacBook Pro and Linux VPS.

Symmetric-function reduction · V2k

A canonical augmented-monomial symmetric-function evaluator replaces V2i’s placement recursion. Lean proves general equivalence with V2i’s placement recursion and verifies exact coefficient tables, including an 85-coefficient piece demonstration. Its 36-statement Comparator suite was accepted on Mac and Linux VPS.

Staged symmetric-function certificates · V2ℓ

Lean proves that certified step chains, chunks, and merges recover V2k’s evaluator and V2i’s placement value. Factor-order invariance permits prefix reuse; certified profiles give a 231-moment representation for the degree-12 Table 3 piece (6,3). The 35-statement Comparator passed on the Linux VPS.

Certified prefix-trie evaluation · V2m

Lean certifies 77 nonroot prefix-trie nodes and 78 reusable moment profiles, with equality to V2i’s placement evaluation for arbitrary moment oracles. Kernel-checked degree-40 coefficient tables include one Table 3 piece (6,3); the remaining rational moments and trie nodes are future work. The 20-statement Comparator passed independently on the Linux VPS.

Direct marked-profile transitions · V2n

Ordinary profiles alone are insufficient for continuing shifted power-sum transitions; a higher-order marked profile is sufficient, with equality tested against every function on marked keys. On Table 3 piece (6,3), Lean certifies 34 direct nodes, including 25 new factor multisets, and covers all 52 multisets in the |λ| ≤ 8 block. Two coefficient tables each use 489 moment keys. The 19-statement Comparator passed on Linux.

What one factor reads · V2o

For factors of degree ≤ d, Lean proves that the parent profile plus binomial summaries δ₁…δ d suffice; monomial-factor responses determine these summaries in characteristic zero. One-level certificates cover 66 three-factor multisets of degree ≤ 26 at Table 3 piece (6,3), forming no canonical leaf states. Combined V2m–V2o coverage is 157 of 2,361 multisets at that piece, with a 574-key coefficient table and Linux acceptance of the eight-statement Comparator.

02 / Verified mathematics

A verified implication and its research pathway

DHL[45,2]  ⟹  H 1 ≤ 212 Lean-verified implication under the stated DHL[45,2] hypothesis.

V2f–V2h certify the symmetric 2×2 marginal Gram block on {1, P₂}. V2i gives exact rational representations for every B₂₁ generator pair; V2j–V2m develop shared moments, symmetric-function evaluation, staged certificates, and prefix-trie reuse. V2n certifies direct marked-profile transitions. V2o proves which binomial read summaries suffice and are determined by degree-bounded monomial responses, adding 66 one-level three-factor certificates. 03 / Exact marginal Gram block

Three certified entries, one polynomial pair

For the functions 1 and P₂ = ∑ᵢxᵢ², the marginal bilinear form has a rational 2×2 Gram block. Decimal values below are readable approximations of the exact rational certificates.

G J on (1, P₂)
1.11935 × 10 −55 4.99944 × 10 −57 4.99944 × 10 −57 2.27107 × 10 −58
The numerical Cauchy–Schwarz ratio J T [P₂,1]² / (J T [1,1] · J T [P₂,P₂]) ≈ 0.984. The matrix entries are certified individually; a separate exact determinant-positivity certificate is a further target.

V2h handles diagonal and distinct-coordinate placements. V2i generalizes the placement construction to products of arbitrary power sums and certifies further Table 3 entries: JT[P₄,1] ≈ 2.75169 × 10−59, JT[P₍₂,₂₎ (= P₂²),1] ≈ 2.28799 × 10−58, and JT[L−P₁,1] ≈ 5.52809 × 10−57. These approximations correspond to exact rationals in the Lean certificates.
