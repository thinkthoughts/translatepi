Encoding · Machine · Rate

The checked proof chain

  1. Encoding correspondence. Convert ParityLifts' normalized 3-CNF inputs to OpenAI's exact canonical CNF bit encoding, preserving satisfiability, keeping the variable count bounded, and proving bit-for-bit equality with OpenAI’s encodeCNF (canonicalWord_eq). Conversion time is polynomial in the variable bound N (canonicalWordProgram_time_poly).
  2. Machine translation. Represent OpenAI's primitive multitape machine in ParityLifts' finite-alphabet machine model with step-level correspondence.
  3. Executable simulation. Construct a fixed ParityLifts FCode runner and prove polynomial resource overhead, yielding simulationHypothesis_proved.
  4. Rate comparison. Bound OpenAI’s subexponential factor 2^(A · runtimeScale N) by an arbitrarily small positive exponential rate (runtimeFactor_expBound); incorporate polynomial converter and simulator costs using sat_code_rate, and refute PositiveRateETH with original_sat_main.

Formal scope

What the results establish

The first kernel-checked conclusion is the negation of the specifically defined ParityLifts predicate PositiveRateETH. That predicate quantifies over deterministic finite-alphabet multitape machines deciding 3-SAT on ParityLifts' binary formula encoding, with a polynomial input-length factor and a positive exponential variable-count rate.

The additional RPI-ETH-010 theorem proves that this negation implies Anthropic’s formal predicate MainResults.ETHIsFalse. Together they provide a checked route to that Anthropic statement, while the cross-formalization proof itself does not import either project’s existing SAT/ETH proof.

These results connect formal definitions; they do not yet establish a two-way equivalence with standard textbook ETH. The converse remains open in this project. OpenAI’s original paper and its precise Lean theorem have distinct complexity bounds; the first bridge uses the Lean theorem.

Review focus: exact definition unfolding; machine and encoding faithfulness; quantifier order; rate preservation; and independent reproduction of the final kernel checks.
